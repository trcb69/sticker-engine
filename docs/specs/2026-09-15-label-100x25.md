# Label 100×25 mm, safe margin, inline line-art logo

Status: spec, Revision 2. Date: 2026-09-15.
Baseline: dev clone `main` at `db86ced`, which is also live.
Rev 2 applies plan-critic's APPROVE-WITH-CHANGES: C1, C2, C4, C5 and C6, plus N1, N2, N4 and N6.
C3 ("logo ring touches border") was checked and is **incorrect**. Hex row 70/71 of `R:LOGOKO.GRF` is `180001…`, so ink starts at column 3, and the PNG ink bbox is (3,3)–(141,141). At x=14 the ring's first dot is 17, a 3-dot gap from a border at 12–13. A test now pins that gap (AC4).

## Problem

Hardware evidence from the GC420t, 2026-09-14 and 2026-09-15 (photos in `/root/Sticker gen/`):

1. **The label stock is 100 × 25 mm**, measured by the human. At 8 dots/mm that is **800 × 200**. The template is 4 × 1 in = **812 × 203** (`src/template/label-4x1.json:4-5`, `src/template/schema.js:244-245`).
2. **The printer lands about 1–8 dots up and left.** On the edge test the ticks at x=0 and y=0 were missing, the ticks at x=8 and y=8 printed, and every right and bottom tick printed. The border at 4–7 dots never shows its top or left side.
3. **The logo prints blank.** The template recalls `R:LOGO.GRF` (`^XG`). The Browser Print path never sends it; only the network path does (`src/net/printer.js:196-226`). `R:` is RAM.
4. **The solid logo fills in.** The line-art logo printed clean with `^GFA` on this printer (2026-09-15 edge test). This proves inline `^GFA` works on the GC420t.
5. **The barcode has no real quiet zone** (calibration B1). The plan reserves 20 dots each side (`src/render/symbology.js:53`), and the preview draws the bars after it (`public/js/render-canvas.js:375`). The ZPL starts the bars at `el.x`.

## Decisions (human, 2026-09-15)

- **D1** The label is 100 × 25 mm.
- **D2** Safe margin: every element, the border included, stays inside the 1.5 mm quiet zone (12 dots at 203 dpi). There is no per-printer offset.
- **D3** The line-art logo, with "STANDARD HOLDINGS" lettering one dot bolder.
- **D4** The logo goes inside every label as uncompressed `^GFA`. Nothing is stored on the printer.
- **Deferred:** the link shortener on `https://standard-holdings.lk` (`STICKER_SHORT_BASE`). `HTTPS://STANDARD-HOLDINGS.LK/J/XXXXXX` is 37 characters, against a QR ceiling of 38.

### Schema `src/template/schema.js`

- A `graphic` slot requires `x, y, w, h` and **exactly one** of `source` or `data`. Messages:
  - `slot "<id>" (graphic) needs either "source" or "data", not both`
  - `slot "<id>" (graphic) needs either "source" or "data"`
- `data` needs:
  - `bytesPerRow === ceil(w/8)`
  - `hex` matching `/^[0-9A-F]+$/`, with length `2·bytesPerRow·h`

  Otherwise: `slot "<id>" graphic data does not match its w×h`.
- `resolve()` scales `w/h` but not `data`, the same as `^XG` today. A resolved template is never re-validated; grep confirms nothing calls `validateTemplate` on a resolved one.

## Acceptance criteria

AC1–AC8 and AC11 are enforced by `npm test`.

- **AC1** `dimensions(resolve(raw, dpi))`:
  - 203 dpi → `{800, 200, 12}`
  - 300 dpi → `{1182, 296, 18}`
  - 600 dpi → `{2365, 591, 35}`

  The ZPL has `^PW800 ^LL200`, `^PW1182 ^LL296` and `^PW2365 ^LL591`. `validateTemplate(raw).id` is `label-100x25-v2`.
- **AC2** At 203, 300 and 600 dpi, with `referenceContext`, `longNameContext` and `noDocNoContext`: `guard(placed)` has no `EDGE_OVERFLOW` or `QUIET_ZONE_INTRUSION`, and no placed element has `edge: true`. No `BARCODE_TOO_NARROW` either, with one exception.
  - *Implementation note (Rev 2.1):* `noDocNoContext` at 203 dpi (quantity `1.000KG`) already raises `BARCODE_TOO_NARROW` on the **old** template: 302 dots against 308 needed. The new geometry gives it 305.
  - That label is flagged in the preview but still prints (code-reviewer W4). So the test requires no `BARCODE_TOO_NARROW` for the reference and long-name contexts at every dpi. For `noDocNoContext` at 203 it requires only that the barcode is no narrower than on `db86ced`. Closing the last 3 dots would take a 1-dot-margin layout; that is left as a follow-up.
- **AC3** Reference barcode at 203:
  - The `^BC` origin x = `batch.x + plan.quietZone`.
  - `plan.moduleWidth` = 2.
  - `batch.x + 2·plan.quietZone + plan.barWidth ≤ batch.x + batch.w ≤ 800 − 12`.
- **AC4** Logo:
  - (a) The reference ZPL has exactly one `^FO14,28^GFA,2592,2592,18,<5184 hex>^FS` and no `^XG`.
  - (b) The hex bits equal the PNG dots, where the expected bits are built in the test from `decodePng` grey values thresholded at < 128 (not via `toGraphicCommand`).
  - (c) The hex popcount is 9.6% ± 0.1% of 20736.
  - (d) The leftmost set bit is column ≥ 3, so logo ink starts at x ≥ 17, at least 3 dots right of the border's inner edge (x 13).
- **AC5** Schema:
  - The shipped template validates.
  - A graphic with both `source` and `data` is rejected, and so is one with neither.
  - A graphic is rejected if its hex length ≠ 2·bytesPerRow·h, if bytesPerRow ≠ ceil(w/8), or if its hex has lowercase or non-hex characters.
  - A graphic with only `source` validates and emits `^XG`.
  - `resizableAxes` returns `{false,false}` for the logo slot.
- **AC6** The reversed-caption rules (`2026-09-14-reversed-bold-text` AC1–AC7) hold at the new coordinates. The "nothing inside a reversed bar" test still runs at 203, 300 and 600 dpi.
- **AC7** The border is `^FO12,12^GB775,176,2^FS` and is still the last field.
- **AC8** `testLabel('x')` from `public/js/testLabel.js` declares `^PW800 ^LL200`. Every `^FO` origin is ≥ 12 from the left and top. The barcode origin y + 60 + 20 (HRI) is ≤ 188.
- **AC9 Emulator evidence** (script and output in `docs/specs/2026-09-15-label-100x25/`): made-up context, and made-up with a long name, through Labelary 8dpmm 3.937×0.984 in, with the logo swapped for a same-size outline box so the company mark stays off third parties.
  - No ink in the outer 12 dots except the border.
  - The first bar is ≥ 20 dots right of the MNF/EXP ink.
  - Also: `logo-local.png`, a local rendering of the template's logo hex, for the human to look at.
- **AC10 Hardware** (the human): print **10 consecutive** RSMSO26090053 labels on the GC420t. Label to label:
  - all four border sides are visible
  - the logo lettering is readable
  - the white name and QTY are readable
  - a phone or scanner reads the barcode
  - nothing is cut at any edge
- **AC11** `npm test` is fully green.

## Risks

- **The printer shift leaves about 4 dots of physical margin top and left.** Label-to-label drift beyond that would clip the border. AC10's 10-label run shows it.
- **`metrics.js` width estimates.** AC9 measures the real barcode gap on the emulator.
- **Tighter spacing:**
  - header → name bar: 3 dots
  - name bar → barcode: 3 dots
  - QR ink → border: 16 dots
  - bars: 40 dots tall
- **Long batch codes or quantities** narrow the barcode. `BARCODE_TOO_NARROW` flags this in the preview as an error, but **printing is not blocked**: the HTTP print path never calls the guard (`src/http/routes/jobs.js:150-193`), which predates this change. A quantity string of 14 characters or more (e.g. `QTY :- 1.000KG`) hits it at 203 dpi.
- **300/600 dpi.** The design is exactly on the quiet-zone limit at 203 (border right edge 787 against 788, bottom 188 against 188). AC2 runs all three densities.
